# Code of Conduct

Glasscode follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its text applies in full; this page says in short what it asks and how to report.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume that the other person means well.
- Talk about the code and the idea, not about the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and that a maintainer decides when a discussion does not end.
- No harassment, no insults, no sexual language or imagery, and nobody's private information
  without their permission.

This holds in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other place of the project, and
wherever someone speaks for it.

## Reporting

Write to a maintainer in private: the people of the
[glasscode-ide organization](https://github.com/orgs/glasscode-ide/people) are the maintainers,
and GitHub's report function (the "…" menu of a comment, an issue or a profile) reaches GitHub's
own staff. Do not report conduct in a public issue.

A maintainer who is part of the matter does not decide on it. Reports are kept private. A
maintainer may remove or edit a comment, an issue or a pull request, and may block a person from
the project for a time or for good.
